There are two kinds of users: Patient and Caregiver.
Patients can:
    manage their personal profile (view, add, edit, delete)
    manage their settings (preferences)
    in privacy settings, they can set what data caregivers can only access

    go to help and support and search for or scroll through FAQs
    
    manage their med tracker (view, add, edit, delete, confirm taken meds)
    manage their appt tracker as well (view, add, edit, cancel, finish appts, and add notes)

    receive reminders from caregivers and tracker events


Caregivers can:
    only manage what their patient only allowed them to
    they can view their patients' personal profile
    they cannot manage their patients' settings
    
    go to help and support and search for or scroll through FAQs


*the use cases above only focus on offline features although caregivers cannot really perform actions without internet*

below are the use cases that will rely on internet:
Patients:
    can link to one caregiver and view their personal profile
    enter a question in help and support and get a suggested faq based on that
    receive real time manual reminders from caregivers

Cargivers:
    can link to many patients
    send manual reminders to their patients (message and the med entry or appt entry they want their patient to be reminded)
